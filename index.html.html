About the Project

Two AI Engines,
One Smart Coach

FitMind AI combines two cutting-edge technologies to deliver truly personalized fitness guidance — no generic plans, no guesswork.

Engine 01

Machine Learning
Classification

A rule-based ML model analyzes your biometric inputs — age, weight, height, activity level, and fitness goal — to classify you into a precise fitness tier: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

  • BMI & body composition analysis
  • Activity level scoring
  • Goal-to-tier mapping
  • Dynamic re-classification
Input Data ML Model Tier Output
Engine 02

Generative AI
Workout Creator

Based on your ML-classified tier, a Generative AI engine synthesizes a complete weekly workout plan — including exercises, sets, reps, rest periods, and calorie estimates — tailored to your exact goal.

  • Dynamic plan generation
  • Goal-specific exercise selection
  • Calorie burn computation
  • Recovery & tip generation
